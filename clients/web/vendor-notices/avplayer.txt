The license texts accompany these assets as COPYING.LGPLv3 and COPYING.GPLv3.
watchparty modifies subtitle-queue reset ordering during seek. The corresponding
source change accompanies these assets as avplayer-seek.patch.

AVPlayer includes assjs and ass-compiler, licensed under MIT.
Their original licenses accompany the assets as ASSJS-LICENSE and ASS-COMPILER-LICENSE.
Source: https://github.com/weizhenye/ASS

The optional HEVC/AAC software playback modules are the upstream 32-bit
WebAssembly builds from the same revision, under dist/decode (hevc and aac),
dist/resample and dist/stretchpitch. They are served from this origin on demand.
FFmpeg provides the video/audio decoders and audio resampler. SoundTouch provides
the audio time-stretch module. Their LGPL-2.1 license text accompanies the modules
in codecs/COPYING.LGPL-2.1. See the pinned upstream README_en.md and build
scripts for the component sources and notices. The accompanying
avplayer-local-buffer.patch describes watchparty’s local-file packet-buffer changes.

watchparty stops the MSE mux timer when its track's SourceBuffer is detached.
The corresponding source change accompanies these assets as avplayer-mse-detach.patch.
